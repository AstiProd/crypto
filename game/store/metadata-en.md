## Long description

Welcome to Kepler-442 b. You are the only astronaut aboard a floating mining
platform — and this base is begging to grow.

⛏️ MINE
Your drills tear energy crystals out of the ground. Pick them up: they stack higher
and higher on your back.

🏭 REFINE
Pour your haul into the refinery, collect the credit chips from the output tray and
cash them in at the terminal.

🔩 MERGE
Drag one drill onto another of the same rank and they become a single, faster,
nearly twice-as-profitable machine. Ten ranks to uncover, from the second-hand Mk-I
to the quantum Mk-X.

🛰️ EXPAND
Unlock new sockets, buy thruster boots, a bigger backpack, a faster refinery — and
up to four worker drones that keep going even while you're away.

✨ WHAT'S INSIDE
• A fully animated space base, from the thrusters under the platform to the alien flora
• Continuous progression: the base produces while you're offline
• No login, no account, everything saved on your device
• Play for a minute or an hour: one merge, or one whole new rank

Ready for lift-off?
